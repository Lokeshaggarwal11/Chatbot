2. Appraisal Policy Introduction: This policy outlines the performance appraisal process for all employees of Quokka Labs. The performance appraisal process aims to provide feedback to employees about their job performance, recognize their achievements, and identify areas for improvement to help employees grow and develop their skills. Performance Appraisal Process: The performance appraisal process will occur annually and will consist of the following steps: Evaluation Criteria: Evaluation criteria will be established for each job position within the department. These criteria will be used to evaluate employees' performance during the appraisal process. The criteria will be based on the employee's job responsibilities, goals, and expectations.   Self-Assessment: Employees will be asked to complete a self-assessment before the appraisal meeting. The self-assessment will allow employees to reflect on their performance, identify their strengths, and highlight areas where they would like to improve.   Manager Assessment: Managers will be responsible for assessing their employees' performance. The manager's assessment will be based on the evaluation criteria and will include feedback on the employee's strengths and areas for improvement.   Performance Review Meeting: Employees will meet with their managers to discuss their performance appraisal. During this meeting, the manager will provide feedback on the employee's performance and discuss the results of the appraisal. The manager and employee will work together to create a plan for future development and set goals for the next performance period.   Performance Ratings: Employees will be rated on a five-point scale, ranging from "exceeds expectations" to "unsatisfactory," based on their overall performance during the appraisal period. These ratings will be used to determine compensation adjustments, promotions, and other career development opportunities.   Follow-up Actions: After the performance appraisal meeting, the manager and employee will work together to develop an action plan to address areas for improvement and support the employee's development and growth. This action plan will include specific goals and objectives, timelines, and metrics for measuring progress.   Performance Improvement Plan: If an employee receives an "unsatisfactory" rating, they will be placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP). The PIP will outline specific areas for improvement, timelines for improvement, and support and resources available to help the employee succeed. Failure to make progress on the PIP may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.  *If employee consistently exceed expectations and demonstrate exceptional performance, they may be eligible for early promotion, rewards, or increments even before the standard one-year evaluation period.

1. Performance Review Introduction: This policy outlines the performance review process for all employees of Quokka Labs. The performance review process aims to recognize and reward employees who consistently perform at a high level and identify areas for improvement to help employees grow and develop their skills. Policy Statement: Performance reviews will be conducted annually for all employees. The performance review process is designed to evaluate the employee's performance, provide feedback, and identify areas for improvement. Performance Review Process: The performance review process will be as follows: Setting goals: At the beginning of the performance review cycle, the employee and their supervisor will set goals that the employee will work towards during the year.   Mid-year review: Halfway through the performance review cycle, the employee and their supervisor will meet to discuss the employee's progress towards their goals.   End-of-year review: At the end of the performance review cycle, the employee and their supervisor will meet to conduct a formal review of the employee's performance. The supervisor will provide feedback on the employee's strengths and areas for improvement, and together they will set goals for the following year.   Confidentiality: All performance review information will be treated as confidential and will not be shared with anyone outside of the IT department without the employee's written consent. Training: All supervisors responsible for conducting performance reviews will receive training on the performance review process and how to provide constructive feedback to employees.

3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Policy Purpose: The purpose of this policy is to establish a fair and consistent process for addressing performance concerns and supporting employees in improving their job performance. Scope: This policy applies to all employees of Quokka Labs who are not meeting performance expectations. Policy: The company is committed to providing its employees with the necessary resources and support to help them achieve their job responsibilities effectively. In cases where an employee's job performance falls below the required standards, the company will initiate a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP is a formal and structured process that outlines specific performance goals and the necessary steps to be taken to achieve them. The employee and their manager will work together to develop the PIP, which will include the following components: 1. Performance Expectations: The PIP will outline the specific performance expectations that the employee must meet to improve their job performance. These expectations will be measurable, achievable, and relevant to the employee's role.   2. Timeline: The PIP will specify a timeline for achieving the performance expectations outlined in the plan. The timeline will be reasonable and take into account the complexity of the job responsibilities and the employee's individual circumstances.   3. Support and Resources: The company will provide the necessary support and resources to help the employee achieve the performance expectations outlined in the PIP. This may include additional training, coaching, mentoring, or other resources as deemed necessary.   4. Performance Review: The employee's manager will regularly review the employee's progress against the performance expectations outlined in the PIP. The review will be conducted according to the timeline specified in the plan.   5. Consequences: If the employee fails to meet the performance expectations outlined in the PIP, the company may take disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. The decision to terminate employment will be made on a case-by-case basis and in accordance with the company's policies and procedures.

Step 2: Disciplinary Hearing This process should be closed within 10 working days, starting from the date the investigation report is submitted by the Disciplinary Committee. The associate will be notified to appear for the disciplinary hearing at least 3 working days before the hearing. The associate under investigation may file a written submission and/or provide Disciplinary committee with the names of witnesses his/her support before two working days of the hearing. At the hearing, the associate will have the opportunity to be heard and an opportunity to defend. This opportunity shall include their response to the allegations, challenge the findings of the investigation, ability to ask questions and seek clarifications, present evidence, call witnesses and be examined during questioning. The proceedings shall be conducted in a fair manner, without any personal biases and ability to ask questions and seek clarifications, present evidence, call witnesses and be examined during questioning. The proceedings shall be conducted in a fair manner, without any personal biases and prejudices against any of the parties to the hearing. If an associate fails to attend a disciplinary hearing upon notice (maximum three times) without any good reason, it may be treated as violation in itself. Even after repeated reminders, if the associate is persistently unable to attend, a decision shall be taken in their absence based on the available evidence.  Step 3: Outcome of the Disciplinary Matter The Disciplinary Committee will consider all the facts and circumstances to decide whether there has been a violation. They will base their decision on the evidence that has been presented. Action on the outcome of the hearing should be taken within 3 working days of the Disciplinary hearing wherein the Committee will inform the employee of its decision in writing.
